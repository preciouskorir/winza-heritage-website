About Dr Winani Thebele

Heritage is about people, memory and belonging.

Dr Winani Thebele's research and professional interests include African heritage, museum collections, colonial histories and debates about restitution and shared heritage.

Her work offers opportunities to consider how collections held by museums can be understood in relation to the communities, histories and cultural knowledge from which they emerged.

Through research, public conversations and heritage collaborations, these questions connect historical collections with contemporary discussions about identity, access, representation and cultural responsibility.
