The story behind Winza Heritage

Celebrating identity and connection.

Winza Heritage celebrates the stories carried through textiles, design, adornment and cultural knowledge. Our approach brings heritage into conversation with contemporary life.

Through our collections and collaborations, we aim to encourage cultural appreciation and create meaningful connections between people, traditions and communities.

We welcome people who share an interest in heritage, creative expression and respectful cultural exchange.

Respecting heritage. Encouraging understanding. Connecting stories.
